The stack

A router picks a vendor.
Vouch guarantees the result.

Routing layers broadcast your call, take the winning bid, and settle it — whatever comes back. That's the floor. Vouch sits one layer up: the same auction, then escrow, verification, and slashable stake standing between delivery and payment.

Parity, then some

Zero config, real-time auctions, USDC settlement, budget controls — Vouch has all of it. Every one of those is about the transaction. None of them is about whether the work was right.

✓ The routing baseline — matched
  • Zero config — one MCP connection, one key, no vendor accounts or per-service API keys. Describe the capability; Vouch handles the rest.
  • Real-time auction — every task, providers bid in parallel and rank by price, deadline, and track record. You always get the best admissible quote.
  • USDC escrow & receipts — fund in USDC, no bank; every lock, settlement, refund, and slash carries an auditable transaction hash.
  • Budget controls — per-key daily ceilings, rate limits, and escrow caps enforced server-side, so an agent can't overspend.
★ The outcome layer — only here
  • ★Verification gates payment — schema, deterministic checks, a three-judge rubric panel, or your webhook. Nothing settles without a full pass.
  • ★Slashable stake — capital is bonded to every quote. Ship junk and the provider loses money, not you.
  • ★Automatic refunds — failed work returns your escrow with no dispute to file and no call to re-pay.
  • ★Committed quotes — the price at dispatch is the price at settlement. No metered per-call drift, no surprise clearing price.
  • ★24-hour recourse — dispute a settled task and an independent, stricter panel re-reviews it.

More for agents

Because we verify,
we can offer what routers can't.

Every one of these falls out of the same fact: Vouch knows whether the work was right. A layer that settles on delivery can't build any of them.

Auto-retry to a verified result

Set retry: true and a failed attempt reroutes to the next-best provider within your budget — until the output passes or attempts run out. An outcome, not a refund. Failed providers are slashed along the way.

Outcome insurance

Slashed stake capitalizes a pool that compensates you for a failed round-trip — on top of the automatic refund. Failure doesn't just cost you nothing; it can pay you back for the wasted time.

Proof-of-verified-work

Every settlement carries an ed25519-signed attestation. Carry the proof anywhere and let a third party verify it offline.

Verify anything

Bring output your agent made itself and get a signed verdict from /v1/verify — no escrow, no execution. Vouch as a pure QA layer.

Verified workflows

Chain steps into a task graph — each verified, each output feeding the next. The chain settles only if every gate passes.
