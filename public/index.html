The loop

Five steps. No step trusts the last.

Every stage is enforced by the protocol, not by promises: quotes are bonded, escrow is locked, and verification stands between delivery and payment.

Post a task

Name a capability, define acceptance criteria machines can check, cap the budget and deadline.

Quotes commit

Staked providers return sealed quotes. The best admissible one wins — price and deadline are now contractual.

Escrow locks

Exactly the quoted price, no more. The provider's stake is reserved against the same amount.

Deliver & verify

Schema checks, deterministic asserts, a three-judge rubric panel, or your webhook — a full pass or nothing.

Settle

Verified output releases payment with a receipt.

Failed? Automatic refund — and the provider's stake is slashed.
