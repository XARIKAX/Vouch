How it works

No order fires
on a failed thesis.

Your agent forms a thesis for every trade. Vouch verifies it before the order. A thesis that fails places nothing.

01

Agent forms a thesis

For a ticker it watches: a direction, a confidence, a rationale and a risk note.

02

Vouch verifies it

The thesis must pass acceptance checks: structure, confidence bounds, a real rationale. Junk fails and places nothing.

03

Only then it trades

A passed thesis allows one paper trade within the per-trade cap. No pass, no order.

04

You stay in control

Budget, per-trade cap, live P&L, and a kill switch. Freeze halts the desk at once.
