How it works

The trade that can't fire
on a bad idea.

Robinhood lets an agent place the order. Vouch verifies the reasoning first — the difference between an autonomous desk and a reckless one.

01

Agent forms a thesis

For a ticker it's watching: a direction, a confidence, a rationale, a risk note.

02

Vouch verifies it

The thesis must clear acceptance checks — structure, confidence bounds, a real rationale. Junk is rejected and the provider is slashed.

03

Only then it trades

A verified thesis unlocks one paper trade within the per-trade cap. No verified thesis, no trade.

04

You stay in control

Budget, per-trade cap, live P&L, and a freeze switch that halts the desk instantly.
