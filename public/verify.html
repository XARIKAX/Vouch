Every settled task ships a signed receipt. Paste one below and its ed25519 signature is checked in your browser against Vouch's public key. Don't trust us. Prove it.

The public key is at /v1/attestation/key. The signed message is the canonical JSON of the payload, the same offline check anyone can run. Pull a real receipt from GET /v1/tasks/{id}/attestation.
